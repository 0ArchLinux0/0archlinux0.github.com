Welcome. This site is a working archive of things I learn, build, test, and try to explain clearly.

If you found the site through an old algorithm post, the archive is still here. The current direction is broader: **software systems, mathematics, research, AI tooling and automation, and selected security/cryptography topics**.

## Where to begin

- **Latest writing:** return to the [home page](/) for the newest posts.
- **Selected work:** see [Work](/work/) for a small set of public projects and research artifacts.
- **By subject:** browse [Categories](/categories/) or [Tags](/tags/).
- **Older material:** use the [Archives](/archives/). Older posts are kept for continuity and may not reflect my current writing style or technical standards.
- **Who I am:** see [About](/about/).

## How I want this site to work

I do not want this to be a résumé copied into blog form. The useful part should be the work itself: reproducible notes, explanations, code, research artifacts, and concrete experiments.

When a project grows beyond a single post, I link the article, source code, and any related research or product page so that the site becomes a map of the work rather than just a chronological feed.
